How the Platform Works

1
Verified Trade Policy RAG — The AI is grounded in a curated knowledge base of US trade policy facts: Section 301 rates, the April 2025 "Liberation Day" actions (145% China effective rate), 90-day pauses for Vietnam (46%) and Bangladesh (37%), and USMCA exemptions for Mexico. This is not generic AI — it reasons from verifiable, sourced policy data.
2
Live News Signal (optional) — When a NewsAPI key is configured, recent trade headlines are injected into the AI prompt, letting the model react to breaking policy signals in real time — the same way a Kalshi prediction market reflects new information.
3
Prediction Market Probabilities — Claude 3.5 Sonnet (AWS Bedrock) reasons over policy history and news to output probability-weighted tariff scenarios: Optimistic (P10), Moderate (P50), and Severe (P90). Think of it as a prediction market contract — the AI is placing probability bets on each outcome, backed by a structured reasoning chain your team can audit.
4
Landed Cost Impact by Category — Each scenario's tariff delta is applied to category-specific China sourcing exposure (china_pct × import_factor × delta) to compute landed cost impact in percentage points across 6 apparel categories.
5
Margin Erosion Model — impact × (1 − retail_margin) translates landed cost impact into margin erosion — the number your P&L team needs. Shown in real time in the banner, KPI row, and Order Commitment tab.
6
Order Commitment Decision Engine — Enter your specific order parameters (category, units, cost, margin) and the platform instantly models your absorb vs. pass-through options across all three scenarios, plus sourcing alternatives by origin. Built to support purchase order decisions before the Spring 2027 commitment deadline.
